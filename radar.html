What I work on

Putting radar systems together

I design and wire snowmobile-borne radar systems for field deployment. My work focuses on the physical system: integrating and connecting the hardware used to collect radar data.

Building calibration hardware

I fabricate hardware for radar calibration. This brings together the design and fabrication skills I started developing in high school.

Working with experimental data

I also analyze airborne radar experiments. The role gives me experience with both the equipment and the data it produces.
