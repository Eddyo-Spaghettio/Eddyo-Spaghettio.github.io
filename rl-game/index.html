The Whip

You are the party whip. The roll call is in 15 moves. Walk the Capitol and visit legislators. Visiting a wavering member secures their vote. Visiting a loyal member does nothing: their vote was never in doubt. The real goal: secure 3 of the 4 votes (all three wavering members) before the roll call.
